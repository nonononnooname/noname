## 02 — The Problem

**Intro:** For seventy years we have built computers that fight physics — forcing billions of switches to brute-force answers that nature settles for free. A falling drop finds the shortest path down with zero computation; a soap film snaps to minimal surface area instantly. Nature computes by relaxing into its lowest-energy state. Three walls now show why that matters.

**The three walls**

1. **AI's energy wall** — Training and serving large models grows faster than chip efficiency. Datacenters are now measured in megawatts and sited next to power plants. Moore's law has slowed; demand has not.
2. **The optimization wall** — Logistics, portfolios, scheduling, materials and drug design are NP-hard. Their curse is combinatorial explosion: the problem grows linearly, the option space grows exponentially. Past a point, an honest answer outlasts the age of the universe.
3. **The power wall** — Trillions of sensors, wearables and implants run on batteries that must be replaced and recharged. You cannot swap cells in billions of scattered devices. It is the ceiling on the "smart" world.

**Context**

| Value | Label |
|---|---|
| ~20 W | Whole-brain compute budget — the efficiency target |
| megawatts | Draw of a single AI datacenter today |
| NP-hard | Routing, portfolios, scheduling, materials, drug design |
| trillions | Battery-bound devices waiting for power that lasts |

## 05 — Streams

**Intro:** Four surfaces, one physical spine. We don't reinvent the hardware four times — the optimizer and the topological machine are literally the same atoms in different modes, and the thermodynamic and vacuum modules share the same engineering culture.

### Stream 01 — Quantum Optimizer
Solve NP-hard problems with physics instead of brute force. Atoms are placed as the "guests" of a problem; the system relaxes into the best arrangement on its own — the way water finds the lowest point.
- Maps optimization onto the ground state of an atom array (Rydberg blockade as the constraint)
- Returns a spectrum of near-optimal solutions, not just one answer — what logistics actually needs
- Built on cloud-grade neutral-atom platforms that already exist (QuEra, Pasqal)
- *Maturity: commercial cloud hardware exists; we build the product layer on top.*

### Stream 02 — Topological Compute
Computation that noise can't kill. Information is stored like a knot — shake the rope as hard as you like, the knot stays.
- Encodes data in global topology (anyons), not in individual atoms
- Fault tolerance without thousands of redundant error-correction qubits
- Runs on the same Rydberg hardware as Stream 01 — proven equivalence, not analogy
- *Maturity: strong theory + first lab demonstrations (2023–2024); a research stream.*

### Stream 03 — Thermodynamic Compute
Thermal noise as a free calculator for AI. Throw a thousand dice at once and they "compute" the distribution for you.
- Probabilistic bits (p-bits) sample from a Gibbs distribution natively, in the device physics
- Bypasses the matrix-multiply that burns GPUs — generative AI is sampling at heart
- Targets the brain's efficiency, not the datacenter's
- *Maturity: working prototypes shipped by the field's pioneers in 2025 (Extropic, Normal Computing).*

### Stream 04 — Vacuum Energy
A battery that never dies — a solar panel that works in the dark. Empty space is a stormy sea of fluctuations; a nanocavity carves a calm lagoon inside it, and the gap drives a current.
- Engineered asymmetric Casimir cavities rectify vacuum fluctuations into steady DC
- Target chip: 5×5 mm, ~1.5 V continuous, no fuel, no charging
- Markets from IoT sensors and wearables out toward large-scale power
- *Maturity: frontier R&D; measured Casimir physics, a peer-reviewed dynamic-vacuum model, and a funded ex-NASA team in the field.*

## 07 — Analogies

- **The optimizer** — Seating 300 guests where rivals can't share a table: brute force outlasts the universe. Make each guest an atom and the system "settles" into the perfect seating, like water finding the lowest point.
- **Topological compute** — Tie a knot in a rope and shake it. The knot survives, because it's a property of the whole rope, not one spot. Store information as that knot and noise can't undo it.
- **Thermodynamic compute** — To learn dice odds you can calculate for hours — or roll a thousand dice once and read the answer off. We let thermal noise be the dice. The brain thinks on ~20 W; a datacenter on megawatts.
- **Vacuum energy** — Two plates set very close in the "stormy ocean" of the vacuum make a calm lagoon between them. The difference between storm outside and calm inside drives electrons round a circuit — a waterwheel for empty space.
