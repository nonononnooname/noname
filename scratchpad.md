# ATQM — Q-Day Convergence Tracker

## Concept (from whiteboard)
- Horizontal lines = life-frame of each quantum-race company (IBM, Google, MS…).
- Dots on lines = real news each company posts about quantum progress.
- Lines run left→right across time, then project/converge toward Q-Day.
- Right edge: the Q-Day year each source predicts (28 / 29 / 30).
- Bottom line = ATQM. ATQM doesn't invent data — it aggregates the field's own
  milestones and publishes analyses ("разбор") of how each one accelerates Q-Day.
  ATQM publication dots sit under the news they reference, arrow points up.
- ATQM's call: Q-Day = 2029.

## Aesthetic
Light analytical / research-intelligence dashboard. Paper bg, hairline grid,
IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Mono. Companies in muted distinct hues; ATQM = ink line
with red diamonds; Q-Day = red. Restrained, data-dense, scannable.
